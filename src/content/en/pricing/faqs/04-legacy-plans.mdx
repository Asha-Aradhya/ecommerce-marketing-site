---
question: "What are the legacy plans?"
order: 4
---

Legacy plans refer to older hosting plans that were previously offered by Cloudnode but are no longer available to new customers. These include the Start, Grow, and Professional plans, which have since been replaced by our current plans. Existing customers on these plans can continue using them, but pricing and support options may differ from current plans. If you're on a legacy plan and interested in transitioning to a newer plan, please reach out to our [support team](/en/coming-soon/) for guidance on available options.
