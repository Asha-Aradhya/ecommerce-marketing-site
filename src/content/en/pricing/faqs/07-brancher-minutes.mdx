question: "What are free Brancher minutes?"
order: 7
---

Cloudnode Brancher is a powerful tool designed for running parallel integration tests, testing software version upgrades, or performing any other tasks requiring a temporary and isolated server environment. Brancher usage is charged per minute. With all AWS and OpenStack plans, we offer 400 complementary Brancher minutes for free to new subscriptions. This means you will not be charged for Brancher usage until it surpasses the threshold of 400 minutes per month per Cloudnode.
