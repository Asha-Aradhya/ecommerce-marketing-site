Release Cluster 21 March 2024

Cluster Cloudnode is now generally available

After an extended early-access period, Cluster Cloudnode is now generally available. Cluster Cloudnodes spread your application across multiple Cloudnodes with a shared database and storage tier, giving you horizontal scaling and high availability without re-architecting your shop. Pricing and configuration details are available on the Plans & prices page.
